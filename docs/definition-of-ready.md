# Definition of Ready

A user story is Ready when:
- Business objective is understandable.
- User/persona is identified.
- Acceptance criteria are present.
- Main happy path is defined.
- Relevant negative cases are discussed.
- Dependencies are identified.
- Required test data can be created.
- Permissions/roles are clear.
- Story is small enough to complete within the sprint.
- QA can determine how the story will be validated.
